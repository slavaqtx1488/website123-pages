How we handle your data

We collect your name, phone number, car details, issue description, preferred contact method and time to respond, arrange a visit and keep a service history. Details are stored in a server database; a notification containing the request may be sent to the administrator through Telegram. The service administrator has access. We do not add advertising trackers. Technical cookies support administrator sign-in; your language is stored in your browser. Contact the service owner using the published details to request access, correction or deletion. The owner sets the retention period and must fill in their legal and contact details before launch.

04 / FAQ

A few more answers.

Can I book if I don’t know what’s broken?+

Yes. Choose symptoms or describe what you noticed. A mechanic will clarify the details and suggest an inspection.

Why is there no fixed repair price?+

The same symptom can have different causes. Cost depends on the vehicle, findings, materials and agreed work. We confirm it before starting.

Do I need an account?+

No. Submit a request without registering. Leave your name, phone number and preferred contact method.

Can I avoid a phone call?+

Yes. Choose Telegram and enter your handle, or choose Viber. A mechanic will use that channel to discuss your request.
